About

It's Me Brian

I'm Brian Do, a software developer with over 5 years of experience, currently residing in Jakarta, Indonesia. I have a deep passion for crafting purposeful interfaces and products. My main goal is to bridge the divide between people and technology, transforming intricate challenges into meaningful and seamless experiences.

More About Me

Brian Do holds a bachelor's degree in Computer Science from a prestigious university in the United States and has a relentless drive for staying up-to-date with the latest technologies and development trends. Actively involved in the developer community, Brian regularly participates in diverse tech conferences and meetups.

When he's not immersed in coding, he finds solace in playing the guitar and exploring new coffee shops in his local area. Brian firmly believes in maintaining a healthy work-life balance, making sure to take breaks and reenergize his creativity. In his spare time, he also volunteers at a local animal shelter on weekends.
